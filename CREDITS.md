# Graves' World 3D: credits and licenses

**Engine**
- [three.js](https://threejs.org) r160 by the three.js authors, MIT License. Loaded, pinned at version 0.160.0, from the jsDelivr CDN
  (`three.module.js`, plus `EffectComposer`, `RenderPass`, `UnrealBloomPass` and `OutputPass` from `examples/jsm`).

**Art, models, textures**
- Every 3D model is built from code at runtime in `world3d.js`: Graves (the hooded necromancer with glowing eyes, staff and lantern),
  the Crypt, Lantern Lane, the Offering Stone, the Bone Garden, the Ledger Shop, trees, gravestones, the wisp, sky and particles.
  No downloaded models or textures are used. Original work for Graves' World. The signs and glow sprites are drawn on a canvas.

**Audio**
- The ambient sound (wind, low drone, chimes) is made live with the Web Audio API in `world3d.js`. No audio files are used.

**Fonts**
- System fonts only.
